02Problem & Opportunity

Security Operations Need Better Aerial Intelligence

Traditional monitoring systems face limitations in visibility, continuity of observation and timely interpretation of complex scenes.

Aerial platforms can provide additional coverage, but maintaining reliable target identity, recovering from temporary visibility loss and presenting actionable information to operators remain significant engineering challenges.

Continuity of Tracking

Maintaining target identity when detections are interrupted or tracker IDs change.

Operational Awareness

Transforming visual detections and tracking events into information operators can understand.

System Integration

Connecting computer vision, mission runtime, monitoring interfaces and future UAV platforms.

06RazorClaw Control Station

A dedicated software application is being developed to visualize target lifecycle, search behavior, off-screen confidence, guidance diagnostics and mission events.

    09Safety & Validation

    Safety-Led Development and Honest Validation

    RazorClaw Systems is currently focused on software research, simulation and controlled validation.

    Shadow guidance is used for diagnostic evaluation and does not represent active UAV control.

    Future hardware integration and field operations will require additional engineering, safety assessment and applicable regulatory approvals.

    Current scope

    • Software research and simulation
    • Controlled validation on recorded video and synthetic scenarios
    • No hardware commands are issued
    • No live UAV flights are claimed

    Requirements for future integration

    • Geo-fencing
    • Manual override
    • Return-to-home
    • Audit logs and role-based access
    • Encryption in transit

    These are requirements, not existing or validated capabilities.

    Compliance

    • Local laws and site policies must be respected
    • Applicable regulatory approvals are required before any field operation
